\chapter*{About this compendium}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this compendium}

This volume binds nine training sheets into one book, ordered as a single
course of study. Parts~I--IV drill the foundations: the algebra of
expressions; functions, their graphs, transformations and the regions they
bound; vector and matrix computation; and the groundwork of analysis
(sequences, series, financial mathematics, limits and continuity).
Parts~V and~VI cover calculus: derivatives in one and several variables,
including L'H\^opital's rule, the complete curve discussion, surfaces and
level curves, Taylor approximation, the gradient, the Hessian and the
Implicit Function Theorem, and then integration. Parts~VII and~VIII apply
this material: static optimization, covering convexity, unconstrained problems,
comparative statics, Lagrange and Kuhn--Tucker, and linear programming
through the simplex method, duality and sensitivity analysis.

Every chapter follows the same discipline: \textbf{Core Rules} state the
rules compactly; \textbf{Practice Problems} drill them; \textbf{Solutions}
work every problem through in full; most chapters close with
\emph{additional problems} for a second pass. Work the problems before
reading the solutions.

Part~IX is different in kind: three \emph{mixed} sets that draw on the
whole sequence without announcing which method each problem wants, since
choosing the method is the skill an examination actually tests, and a
fourth set that runs single functions of two variables through the whole
chain of questions, from domain to a checklist of properties. The book
closes with a formula summary and a German--English glossary, since the
mathematics here is written in English while business and economics degrees
across the German-speaking countries examine it in German.

Each part also exists as a standalone volume; this compendium is assembled
from the same chapter files, so the two presentations never diverge.
