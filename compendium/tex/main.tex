\chapter*{About this compendium}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this compendium}

This volume binds six training sheets into one book, ordered as a single
course of study. Parts~I--III drill the foundations: algebra, vector and
matrix computation, and the groundwork of analysis (sequences, series,
financial mathematics, limits and continuity). Parts~IV and~V cover
calculus: derivatives in one and several variables, including Taylor
approximation, the gradient, the Hessian and the Implicit Function Theorem,
and then integration. Parts~VI and~VII are the payoff: static optimization,
covering convexity, unconstrained problems, comparative statics, Lagrange
and Kuhn--Tucker, and linear programming through the simplex method, duality
and sensitivity analysis.

Every chapter follows the same discipline: \textbf{Core Rules} state the
rules compactly; \textbf{Practice Problems} drill them; \textbf{Solutions}
work every problem through in full; most chapters close with
\emph{additional problems} for a second pass. Work the problems before
reading the solutions.

Each part also exists as a standalone volume; this compendium is assembled
from the same chapter files, so the two presentations never diverge.
