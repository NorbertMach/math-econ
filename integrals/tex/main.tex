\chapter*{About this sheet}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this sheet}

This volume binds two companion drill sets. Part~I is the
\emph{Integration} sheet: it drills the computation of integrals, the
inverse operation of differentiation and the language of accumulation. The
first half covers the mechanics (basic antiderivatives and linearity,
substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions); the second turns to
definite integrals via the Fundamental Theorem, improper integrals, economic
applications such as consumer and producer surplus and present value, and
closes with double integrals.

Part~II is the \emph{Algebra Problem Sheets}, covering the algebraic
manipulation the integration work presumes: exponents and roots, logarithms,
rational expressions, factoring, inequalities, systems of equations,
summation notation, partial fractions, and, looking ahead to the
optimization volumes, the algebra of first-order conditions. Most chapters
of Part~I close with a block of \emph{additional problems} for a second pass
through the material.

Every chapter states its rules first and drills them second, with full
step-by-step solutions rather than bare answers.
