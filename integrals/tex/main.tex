\chapter*{About this sheet}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this sheet}

This sheet drills the computation of integrals, the inverse operation of
differentiation and the language of accumulation. The first half covers the
mechanics (basic antiderivatives and linearity, substitution, integration by
parts, partial fractions); the second turns to definite integrals via the
Fundamental Theorem, improper integrals, economic applications such as
consumer and producer surplus and present value, and closes with double
integrals.

The partial-fraction and summation work this presumes is drilled in
\emph{Algebraic Foundations}; if the algebra is the part that slows you
down, start there.

Every chapter states its rules first and drills them second, with full
step-by-step solutions rather than bare answers. Most chapters close with a
block of \emph{additional problems} for a second pass through the material.
