\chapter*{About this sheet}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this sheet}

This sheet drills static optimization: maximising and minimising functions
of one and several variables, with and without constraints. It runs from
formulating economic optimization problems, through convex sets and
convex/concave functions, unconstrained optimization and the second-order
conditions, comparative statics and the envelope theorem, the method of
Lagrange (first-order conditions, the meaning of the multiplier, multiple
constraints and the bordered Hessian), and finally the Kuhn--Tucker
conditions of nonlinear programming.

The prerequisites are drilled in the companion volumes: partial derivatives,
gradients and Hessians in \emph{Derivatives \& Partial Derivatives};
definiteness of quadratic forms in \emph{Matrix Algebra}; the algebra of
first-order conditions in the \emph{Algebra Problem Sheets}.

The structure is the same in every chapter: \textbf{Core Rules} states the
rules compactly; \textbf{Practice Problems} drills them; \textbf{Solutions}
works every problem through in full. Most chapters close with
\emph{additional problems} for a second pass.
